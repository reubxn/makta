# Matka: 2-minute talk track

About 310 words: two minutes at a calm pace. Slide numbers in brackets. Every number below is on the slides and in the app.

**[1] 0:00**
Same cancer, same treatment. Different distance. Matka shows what radiotherapy costs patients in travel, municipality by municipality, and what shorter schedules would remove.

**[2] 0:10**
637,661 people in 89 municipalities live more than 100 km by road from their unit. Lapland has none: from Utsjoki it is 676 km to Oulu, 9 h 22 min each way. The national roadmap itself says palliative radiotherapy is used less beyond 100 km.

**[3] 0:25**
Distance bites at one step: radiotherapy, where the schedule sets the travel. 15 breast sessions from Utsjoki means 16 nights away.

**[4] 0:35**
For eligible patients, the evidence is there. FAST-Forward, 4,096 patients: 5 sessions in one week is non-inferior to 15 over three. For painful bone metastases, a single 8 Gy session relieves pain as well, with more retreatment, which we count. Yet Matka found a published schedule for 1 of 12 units.

**[5] 0:50**
Matka puts it on a map with a lever, and every number clicks through to its source.

**[6–7] 1:00**
Moving the roughly 1,750 eligible breast patients still on 15 sessions to 5 frees about 17,500 machine sessions a year, and 1,950 nights away. Six counties carry 83% of those nights. The OYS catchment alone carries 40%.

**[8–9] 1:15**
Three changes. 5 sessions as the default for eligible breast patients, with distance named as a reason. A single 8 Gy session for uncomplicated painful bone metastases. And every unit publishes its schedule mix by distance band, every year. Each has an owner, a 30-day first step and a Goal 3 indicator. We ask for a 12-month pilot at OYS.

**[10–11] 1:35**
AI read the units' pages and wrote 22 county memos, but never computes: 264 of 264 memo numbers trace to the facts. The biggest unknown is today's schedule mix, so we show the range, not a point.

**[12] 1:50**
Next step: an introduction to OYS and Lapland, and one year of their data. We can't move the hospital. The schedule can.

## If a judge asks

- **"Is 5 sessions better?"** No: non-inferior, with a similar level of cancer control at 10 years. It applies to whole breast or chest wall without nodal radiotherapy; 15 sessions stays standard when nodes are treated.
- **"Bone: doesn't a single session mean more trips?"** Beyond 100 km, nights away fall from 6.2 to 2.4, but trips stay about the same (2.2 to 2.4) because 20% need retreatment. We show that rather than hide it.
- **"What if units already use 5 sessions?"** Then the gain is smaller: at 50% already on 5 sessions it is about 10,900 sessions a year. Nobody publishes the mix, which is why change 3 exists.
- **"Why OYS?"** It serves Lapland, which has no unit, and has the longest drive in Matka's routing (Utsjoki, 676 km).
